# Credits

## Default pet

The default pet is **Orange Fat Cat** by shuaa ([megupets.com](https://www.megupets.com)), published on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/orange-fat-cat) under [Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The frames were resized and combined into animated PNG files with `scripts/make-apng.swift`.
